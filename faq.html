About our services

What exactly does Verity Life Solutions do?

We are a financial education and coaching firm. We teach the fundamentals of personal and business finance, coach clients through decisions, and — where suitable — help arrange insurance-based strategies through licensed advisors.

Do you provide investment advice or manage investments?

No. We do not sell securities, manage investment portfolios, or provide investment, tax, or legal advice. Our focus is education, coaching, and insurance-based strategy arranged through licensed professionals.

What is "becoming your own banker"?

It's an educational concept built around participating whole life insurance, emphasizing stability, liquidity, and long-term control over your finances. It is a life insurance strategy, not an investment, and it does not guarantee any return. We explain how it works so you can decide whether it fits your goals.

How much does it cost to get started?

Your first, introductory conversation is complimentary and carries no obligation. If you move forward with an insurance strategy, our licensed advisors are compensated by the carrier through standard commissions, disclosed to you in advance. Any coaching fees are agreed in writing before you commit.

Where are you located?

Our office is at 230 · 9715 Horton Road SW, Calgary, AB T2V 2X5. We serve clients across Canada by phone and video as well as in person.

About text messages

Will I receive text messages from you?

Only if you opt in. On our contact form you can optionally check a box to consent to text messages. If you don't check a box, we won't text you.

What kind of text messages will I get?

If you opt in to transactional messages, we may send appointment confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups related to a request you made — for example, a link to book or join a meeting. If you separately opt in to marketing messages, you may also receive occasional updates and offers.

How often will you message me, and are there costs?

Message frequency varies depending on your interactions with us. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

How do I stop messages or get help?

Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or reply HELP for assistance. You can also contact us at 1-587-507-4545.

Will my phone number be shared?

No. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

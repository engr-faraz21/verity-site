Services

What we offer, explained plainly

Everything we do starts with education. We help you understand the concepts, then coach you through decisions and coordinate the details with licensed professionals.

Financial education

Structured, plain-language sessions covering cash flow, debt management, protection, and how money flows through a household or a business. You leave understanding the "why," not just the "what."

One-on-one coaching

Personalized guidance and accountability. We meet you where you are, set realistic next steps, and check in as you go so progress actually happens.

Insurance-based strategies

Where it suits your goals, we explain and help arrange participating whole life insurance strategies (sometimes called "becoming your own banker"). These are life insurance concepts focused on stability, liquidity, and long-term control — not investments, and never a promise of returns. Products are arranged through licensed advisors and carriers.

Professional coordination

We collaborate with your accountant and other trusted professionals so your strategy stays consistent and nothing important falls through the cracks.

How we're compensated

We believe you should understand our business model before you engage us. Transparency here is part of the service.

  • Introductory consultations are complimentary. The first conversation carries no cost and no obligation.
  • Insurance strategies: when you choose to put an insurance-based strategy in place, our licensed advisors are compensated by the insurance carrier through standard commissions. This is disclosed to you before anything is arranged.
  • Coaching engagements: where a dedicated coaching program applies, the scope and any fees are agreed with you in writing in advance. You will never be surprised by a charge.

You will always know what a service involves, and how we are paid, before you decide to move forward.

About us

A steady hand for everyday financial decisions

Verity Life Solutions Ltd. is a financial education and coaching firm in Calgary, Alberta. We believe most people don't need a sales pitch — they need to understand their options and have someone knowledgeable in their corner.

Our approach

We started Verity Life Solutions to do financial guidance differently: education before recommendation, honesty over hype, and relationships that last well beyond a single meeting. We take the time to explain concepts in plain language so that every decision you make is one you actually understand.

Our work centres on personal and business financial coaching and, where suitable, insurance-based strategies arranged through licensed advisors. We coordinate with your other professionals — such as your accountant — so your finances stay organized and consistent.

What we are not: We do not sell securities, we do not manage investment portfolios, and we never promise a rate of return. Any concept we teach, including participating whole life insurance strategies, is shared for educational purposes and implemented only through appropriately licensed professionals.
